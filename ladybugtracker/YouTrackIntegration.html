Authentication

To log in from the plugin you need to generate a token first. Follow these steps to do this

  1. Open user profile
  1. Navigate to the “Account Security” tab
  1. Click “New Token”
  1. Enter any name, and click “Create”
  1. In the plugin use your username and token to login
